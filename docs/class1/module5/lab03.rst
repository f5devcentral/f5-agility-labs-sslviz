Create a New Service Chain
================================================================================

You will create a new **Service Chain** that contains all three **Services**. However, instead of stepping through the **Guided Configuration** wizard, you will go directly to the **Service Chains** object list to create it.


#. Click on the **Service Chains** tab.

#. Click on the **Add** button to create a new **Service Chain**.

   .. image:: ./images/sc2-1.png
      :align: center

   |

#. Enter ``malware_scan`` in the **Name** field.

#. In the **Services Available** list, double-click on **ssloS_wireshark** to add it to the **Selected Service Chain Order** list.

#. In the **Services Available** list, double-click on **ssloS_CLAM_AV** to add it to the **Selected Service Chain Order** list.

#. In the **Services Available** list, double-click on **ssloS_FEYE** to add it to the **Selected Service Chain Order** list.

   |

   .. image:: ./images/sc2-2.png
      :align: center

   |

#. Click on the **Deploy** button.

#. When the deployment completes, click on the **OK** button to return to the **SSL Orchestrator > Configuration** page.

#. Click on the **Service Chains** tab to view the newly created item.

   .. image:: ./images/sc2-3.png
      :align: center
